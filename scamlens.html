What it checks

Six lenses on every message

Each check is a red flag security teams look for. ScamLens shows which ones fired, so users learn to spot them on their own.

Lookalike domains

Brand names in the wrong domain, or letters swapped to fool the eye.

paytrn-secure.com

Hidden characters

Punycode and non-Latin letters that look identical to real ones.

xn--pple-43d.com

Risky link shapes

Shorteners, bare IP addresses, throwaway TLDs and plain http.

http://103.21.4.9/kyc

Pressure language

Deadlines, threats of blocking or disconnection, "act now" wording.

blocked today

Credential requests

Any ask for OTP, UPI PIN, CVV or passwords. Real banks never ask.

share the OTP

UPI traps

Collect requests and QR codes dressed up as refunds. A PIN always sends money.

upi://pay?pa=…
How it works

Forward it the way you'd ask a friend

Forward or paste

Share a message, screenshot, link or QR photo to the ScamLens bot or this page.

Extract and analyze

Links are unwrapped, QR codes decoded, and text scored against known scam patterns.

Get a plain verdict

A risk score, each red flag explained, and one clear action: ignore, report or block.
